It is so beautiful,

The red, orange, yellow, and the grey

of the autumn.

The falling leaves, the cold breeze.

I am here too

watching, feeling, thinking.

This death is indeed the most beautiful one to witness.

The falling of a season,

slow and steady

happening in front of my eyes

it is, yes, very beautiful,

The death in disguise

I am it,

The red, yellow, orange in the grey

laughing, smiling while the inside of me, dying.

I sit here on a cold autumn bench,

the falling leaves pat my head,

orange does look pretty in my grey hands

The orange leaf, In its last stage,

kisses me goodbye.

I love autumn

Orange, red and yellow

the chills of goodbye

I am it

orange red yellow in my greys.
