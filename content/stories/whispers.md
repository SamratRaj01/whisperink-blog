Inside my head

The heart, a burden, weighed with woe, My vision blurred, hands tremble slow.

I sense the weight, a heavy toll.

Pain echoes deep, its source unknown, A solitary figure, cold and alone.

In the vast sea, lost I stand, A soul adrift, on shifting sand.

Shall someone find this castaway? Or should I take a step, find my own way?

Hand trembles, heart in leaden state.

Frozen by the sea.

My body pleads, "Rest a while longer.

Will I be found in this vast sea? Can someone, somewhere, rescue me?

Hello there, lost in the sea's embrace, Seeking solace in this boundless space
